      Lightning near you

      Checking for lightning…

        Gold rings are 10, 25, 50, 100 and 150 km from you. Coloured patches are where the Meteosat weather satellite saw lightning in the last 5 minutes; dots mark each patch’s centre, older ones fade over half an hour. Positions are roughly ±5–10 km, and the data runs a few minutes behind real time. Thunder delays assume sound travels 343 m a second.

        Lightning data: EUMETSAT, MTG Lightning Imager.

        Flash-to-bang timer

        Tap the button when you see lightning, then again when you hear the thunder. Sound covers about 1 km every 3 seconds (1 mile every 5).

          If you can hear thunder you’re close enough to be struck. Go indoors and wait 30 minutes after the last thunder. Don’t rely on this app for safety.

          Sun

            Civil twilight: still light enough to be outside without a torch. Nautical twilight: the horizon fades out at sea and the brightest stars appear. Astronomical twilight: the last glow goes, and once it ends the sky is properly dark.
